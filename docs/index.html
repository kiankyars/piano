Help and playback speed

Both staves have five lines. The top staff is the right hand; the bottom is the left. A rest means that hand waits. These exercises have no finger numbers.

Use “Hear it” after your own attempt to check pitches and rhythm. It uses a simple synthesized sound, with one bar of clicks before the notes. You can play at any comfortable tempo.

If you keep stopping, try a lower level or one hand. Pressing Next changes the exercise, not the level.

2. Pieces — 30 minutes
