# Starting scores

Open the [practice page](https://kiankyars.github.io/piano/): 30 minutes reading, then 30 minutes on your pieces. This optional shortlist removes some searching. Use any suitable unread score; the list is not a daily schedule or rigid difficulty ladder.

Use the first **4–8 bars** of each suggested score initially. You do not need to finish an entire piece. If a selection introduces too many unfamiliar notes or rhythms, use the next suitable standard-staff score in [First Pieces](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebiesFP.html) or [Primer A](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebiesP.html). Skip entries labelled “no staffs” and choose plain notation rather than color-coded/letter-name versions.

| Score | Something to notice |
| --- | --- |
| [Les Cloches](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pLesCloches/pLesCloches.pdf) | Choose a tempo that allows continuity |
| [Goodnight, Ladies](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pGoodnightLadies/pGoodnightLadies.html) | Bass clef and the next hand entry |
| [Five Fat Turkeys Are We](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pFiveFatTurkeys/pFiveFatTurkeys.html) | Count through rests |
| [The Juniper Tree](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pJuniperTree/pJuniperTree.html) | Repeated directions and intervals |
| [Yonder She Comes](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pYonderSheComes/pYonderSheComes.html) | Coordinate hands without losing the beat |
| [Gray Squirrel](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/Primer/pGraySquirrel/pGraySquirrel.html) | One expressive choice on easy music |

These prompts are suggestions, not claims that a score uniquely teaches that feature. For additional excerpts, use other unread scores at the same level. If you know a tune well enough to play by ear, it is still useful reading practice but a weaker benchmark. Occasionally choose a completely unfamiliar title at your current level to check progress.

## The continuing queue

At the end of each session, leave the next unread score open or bookmark it. On the next day:

1. Open your current library: [First Pieces](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebiesFP.html), [Primer](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebiesP.html), [Level 1](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebies1.html) or [Level 2](https://www.gmajormusictheory.org/Freebies/freebies2.html).
2. Work through titles in page order, skipping unfamiliar technical demands that exceed your current stage. The library's ordering is broad, not an exact sight-reading progression.
3. Move to an unread score or passage. A different excerpt of a long piece can supply fresh notation, but repeated musical patterns make it less useful as an independent benchmark.
4. If you exhaust suitable scores, use a graded book or the optional exercise service in [resources](resources.md). Do not move to overly hard music simply because you finished a list.

No need to learn all of these pieces for performance. The purpose is many manageable first encounters with notation.
